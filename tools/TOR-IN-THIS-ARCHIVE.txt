Tor, shipped with the Q21 wallet
================================

This folder holds Tor, from the Tor Project (https://www.torproject.org),
unchanged: the "Tor Expert Bundle" of a stable Tor Browser release. The Q21
wallet starts it when Tor is turned on in the Network tab, and stops it when
the wallet closes. You have nothing to install and nothing to keep open.

Where it comes from
-------------------

The release workflow of Q21 (.github/workflows/release.yml, step "Tor, from
the Tor Project, signature verified") downloads the bundle from
dist.torproject.org and refuses it unless its OpenPGP signature verifies
with the Tor Browser Developers signing key, fingerprint

    EF6E 286D DA85 EA2A 4BA7  DE68 4E2C 6E87 9329 8290

The last line of this file names the version and the platform.

To check it yourself, download the same bundle and its .asc signature from
https://www.torproject.org/download/tor/ and compare the files.

What was left out
-----------------

The pluggable transports (bridges for censored networks) are not included.
Without them Tor connects normally; on a network that blocks Tor, it does not
connect, and the wallet waits instead of connecting in the clear.
